01 / ABOUT

A little background.

I am a postdoc at the Department of Electronics, Information and Bioengineering (DEIB) at Politecnico di Milano, where I work with Prof. Alberto Marchesi.

I received my PhD in computer science at Politecnico di Milano, advised by Prof. Nicola Gatti. During my PhD, I was a visiting researcher at the Industrial Engineering & Operations Research (IEOR) Department at Columbia University, where I worked with Prof. Christian Kroer.

My research focuses on the theoretical aspects of artificial intelligence and online learning. I combine online learning theory with optimization techniques to build strategic agents that can act in complex online environments.
